About This Book

*The Little Prince* is a philosophical novella by French aviator and writer Antoine
de Saint-Exupéry, first published in 1943 during his exile in the United States.
Saint-Exupéry, whose life was deeply intertwined with aviation, infused the book with
a sense of wonder, solitude, and danger drawn from his own flying experiences. The story
follows a young prince from a tiny asteroid who travels across the universe, meeting
eccentric, narrow-minded adults on various planets before landing on Earth, where he
befriends a stranded pilot in the Sahara—a character widely seen as the author's alter
ego. Together, they explore profound truths about love, friendship, and responsibility,
particularly through the prince's devotion to his capricious rose and the wise fox's
lesson that "what is essential is invisible to the eye." Despite its simple, childlike
tone, the novella offers a deep meditation on childhood innocence, the loss of wonder,
and the importance of seeing with the heart.
